Alternatives

A Brow alternative if you want a paid-once Mac menu bar toolkit — not a free everything-app

Brow’s free suite is hard to beat on price and breadth. Mac Kit is a focused Mac menu bar toolkit: fourteen offline tools behind one icon, a 7-day trial, then $9.99 once. Here is the honest trade — including where Brow wins and we do not pretend otherwise.

The short answer

Buy Brow if you want a large free suite (launcher, clipboard, screenshots with OCR/scrolling, window manager, notch widgets, and more) and you are fine exploring a broad surface — or you want Brow Pro’s AI grammar, dictation, and translation on a subscription.

Buy Mac Kit if you want a compact Mac menu bar toolkit: fourteen offline tools in one calm panel, no account, Apple Silicon, and a hard stop on pricing after $9.99 once. Capture and monitor are deliberately shallow; the product is consolidation, not depth-per-category.

Both can coexist. Neither needs to “win” every row.

Where Brow is still the better buy

We would rather you buy the right app. Brow wins clearly on these:

  • Free tier. A real free suite. Mac Kit’s trial ends; Brow’s free features do not.
  • Breadth. Launcher, window management, OCR, scrolling capture, notch hub — wider surface than Mac Kit’s fourteen tools.
  • AI Pro if you want system-wide grammar, dictation, and translation without a separate Grammarly-style bill.
  • Depth in several modules Brow markets as CleanShot/Paste/iStat-class replacements; Mac Kit does not claim that depth.

Where Mac Kit wins

Mac Kit is not trying to be an everything-app. It is selling a finishable toolkit behind one icon:

  • One clear price. $9.99 once for the whole Mac menu bar toolkit — no “Pro” ladder for core tools.
  • Compact panel. Capture, convert, clipboard, focus, and system controls in one scanable menu bar UI — not a second OS of modules.
  • Explicit offline. No cloud product; trial unlocks everything; licence is one Mac, one key.
  • Suite you can finish learning. Fourteen tools, four groups — less discovery tax.

Also consider: Octobar

Octobar is a modular menu bar utility (toggle modules on/off; free up to five modules, Pro unlocks the rest). Apple Silicon, macOS 15+, no account/analytics. Choose Octobar if you want à-la-carte menu bar modules (Pack Menu, Stay Awake, colour picker, snippets, etc.). Choose Mac Kit if you want a fixed Mac menu bar toolkit with capture, clipboard, convert, and focus in one purchase. Optionally, FavTray fits a developer “cockpit” niche (ports, GitHub, AI spend, Dynamic Island) — different job than Mac Kit’s general productivity panel.

Questions people ask

Is Mac Kit a free Brow alternative?

No. Brow’s core suite is free; Mac Kit is a 7-day free trial, then $9.99 once. If “$0 forever” is the requirement, Brow wins that row.

Does Mac Kit include a launcher like Brow?

No. Mac Kit lives in the menu bar as a toolkit panel. It is not an ⌥Space launcher or notch command center.

Can I use Mac Kit and Brow together?

Yes. Some people keep Brow for launcher/OCR/notch and Mac Kit for the compact convert / Screen Draw / Clean Mode / Quick Action set — or pick one to declutter.

Is $9.99 really one-time?

Yes. One payment, one licence key, one Mac, every 1.x update included, no renewal.

Try it before you decide

Try Mac Kit free for 7 days — full Mac menu bar toolkit, no card, no account — then $9.99 once if it earns the icon. If Brow’s free suite already covers you, stay on Brow; we would rather you buy the right app.
