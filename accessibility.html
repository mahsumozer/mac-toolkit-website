Accessibility Statement

Last updated: June 10, 2026. We want Mac Kit and this website to be usable by as many people as possible.

1. Commitment

Mac Kit aims to provide a clear, keyboard-friendly, readable experience for people using different devices, browsers, assistive technologies, and macOS accessibility settings.

2. Standards

We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines as a reference for the website, with the goal of aligning with WCAG 2.1 Level AA where practical for a small product site.

3. Website Measures

  • Semantic HTML structure for headings, navigation, forms, and footer links.
  • Readable contrast choices and responsive layouts for common screen sizes.
  • Form labels for email signup fields.
  • Visible text labels for primary actions and legal links.

4. Product Measures

For the macOS app, we consider keyboard access, readable controls, clear labels, and compatibility with platform accessibility features as part of ongoing product design.

5. Known Limitations

Some visual product previews on the website are illustrative and may not expose every detail as interactive content. We continue to refine labels, contrast, motion, and navigation as the product evolves.

6. Feedback

If you find an accessibility issue or need information in another format, contact us at hello@usemackit.com. Please include the page, feature, browser or macOS version, and the assistive technology involved if relevant.
