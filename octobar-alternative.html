Alternatives

An Octobar alternative if you want a fixed Mac menu bar toolkit, not à-la-carte modules

Octobar is modular: free up to five active modules, Pro $4.99 once for unlimited modules and future ones. Mac Kit is a fixed Mac menu bar toolkit: fourteen offline tools behind one icon, a 7-day trial, then $9.99 once. Here is the honest trade, including where Octobar wins.

The short answer

Buy Octobar if you want to toggle modules on and off (Hide Notch, Pack Menu, Stay Awake, Colour Picker, Shortcuts, snippets, and more), keep a free tier capped at five active modules, or unlock everything with a $4.99 one-time Pro purchase that includes future modules and Family Sharing.

Buy Mac Kit if you want a finished fourteen-tool panel (capture, clipboard, convert, focus, system glance) with one price after trial, no module picker, and no free forever tier for the full set.

Both are Apple Silicon menu bar apps and can coexist. Neither needs to win every row.

Where Octobar is still the better buy

We would rather you buy the right app. Octobar wins clearly on these:

  • Modular free tier. Up to five active modules at $0. Mac Kit’s trial ends; the full toolkit is paid.
  • À-la-carte surface. Hide Notch, Pack Menu, Shortcuts, Connected Devices, Read QR, Translate, and similar mini utilities you turn on only when needed.
  • Pro economics for module collectors. $4.99 once unlocks unlimited modules, future modules, submenu groups, and Family Sharing for up to six people.
  • App Store install path if that is how you prefer to buy and update Mac software.

Where Mac Kit wins

Mac Kit is not trying to be a module shop. It is selling a finishable toolkit behind one icon:

  • Fixed suite. Fourteen tools in four groups - capture, convert, clipboard, focus/system - without deciding which five modules stay on.
  • Capture + convert depth in one panel. Screenshots, Screen Draw, recording, file converter, and PDF↔Markdown sit together; Octobar is stronger on modular utilities than on a capture/convert suite.
  • One clear post-trial price. $9.99 once for the whole Mac menu bar toolkit - no free-cap vs Pro split for core tools.
  • Offline DMG trial. Full tools for 7 days, no card, no account, then a Polar licence key.

Questions people ask

Can I use Mac Kit and Octobar together?

Yes. Some people keep Octobar for toggleable modules (Hide Notch, Pack Menu, Shortcuts) and Mac Kit for the fixed capture / clipboard / convert / focus panel - or pick one to declutter.

Is Mac Kit $9.99 one-time?

Yes. One payment, one licence key, one Mac, every 1.x update included, no renewal. Octobar Pro is also a one-time $4.99 purchase for unlimited modules.

Does Mac Kit do Pack Menu–style modules?

No. Mac Kit ships a fixed fourteen-tool panel. You do not toggle modules on and off or buy a free tier capped at five active modules.

Which needs a newer macOS?

Octobar requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later (Apple Silicon). Mac Kit targets Apple Silicon (M1 or later); check the current download page for the exact macOS floor.

Try it before you decide

Try Mac Kit free for 7 days - full Mac menu bar toolkit, no card, no account - then $9.99 once if it earns the icon. If Octobar’s free five modules already cover you, stay on Octobar; we would rather you buy the right app.
