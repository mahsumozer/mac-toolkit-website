Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026. This policy explains what information Mac Kit may collect and how it is used.

1. Overview

Mac Kit is designed as a local macOS utility. Core app actions such as screenshots, clipboard tools, timers, color picking, and system controls are intended to run on your device.

2. Information You Provide

If you contact us, join an email list, or submit a form, we may receive your email address and the message or details you choose to provide.

3. Payments

If you purchase Mac Kit, payment details may be processed by a third-party payment provider. We do not need to store your full payment card details on this website.

4. App Data

Some app features may access local information only to perform the action you request, such as reading clipboard content for clipboard history or selecting screen content for capture tools. You should avoid using these features with information you do not want stored locally.

5. Website Data

The website does not use advertising or analytics trackers. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps. We use this to keep the site reliable and to prevent abuse.

6. Privacy Choices

We do not use advertising or analytics pixels. This site stores a single value in your browser to remember that you dismissed the privacy notice, and our payment provider sets its own cookies when you open checkout. You can clear this data through your browser settings, unsubscribe from emails at any time, or contact us to manage privacy choices.

7. How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve Mac Kit.
  • To respond to support or contact requests.
  • To send product updates if you sign up for them.
  • To process purchases and prevent abuse or fraud.

8. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help operate the website, email list, payment processing, support, or security.

9. Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails, contact us to request access or deletion where applicable, and control app permissions through macOS settings.

10. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@usemackit.com.
