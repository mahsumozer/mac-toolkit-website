Alternatives

A CleanShot X alternative that also replaces thirteen other apps

CleanShot X is the better pure capture app; Mac Kit is a Mac menu bar toolkit for when capture is one of several jobs you want behind a single icon. Here is the honest version of that trade, with no rounding in our favour.

The short answer

Buy CleanShot X if screen capture is your job — if you need scrolling capture, text recognition inside a screenshot, or a shareable cloud link seconds after you press the shortcut. Nothing here beats it at that, and $29 once is a fair price for it.

Buy Mac Kit if your capture needs stop at “grab an area, mark it up, drop it in Slack” — and you also have a clipboard manager, a Pomodoro timer, a caffeinate app and a system monitor sitting next to it in the menu bar. Same $9.99 covers all fourteen.

Both are one-time purchases from independent developers. Neither is a subscription, and that is the part most of this category gets wrong.

Where CleanShot X is still the better buy

We would rather you buy the right app than buy ours. CleanShot X wins clearly on these, and if any one of them is a daily need for you, stop reading and go buy it:

  • Scrolling capture. Long pages, long chat threads, long invoices in one image. Mac Kit cannot do this at all.
  • OCR. Select text out of a screenshot. Genuinely useful, genuinely absent from Mac Kit.
  • Cloud links. Capture, then paste a URL. If your team lives on shared screenshots, this alone justifies the difference in price.
  • Annotation depth. More shapes, more precision, a proper editing window rather than a quick pass.
  • Intel Macs. Mac Kit is Apple Silicon only. On a 2019 MacBook Pro this comparison is over before it starts.
  • Maturity. Years of releases and a large user base behind it. Mac Kit is 1.x.

Where Mac Kit wins

What Mac Kit is actually selling is not a better screenshot tool. It is one icon instead of ten:

  • Fourteen tools, one purchase. screenshots and recording, Screen Draw, colour picking, Mirror with studio light, file conversion, PDF↔Markdown, new file creation, Pomodoro, keep awake, Clean Mode, clipboard history, system monitor, Macros, and Quick Action.
  • A third of the price, and updates never re-bill. $9.99 once, every 1.x refinement included — no year-two update fee to weigh up.
  • Screen Draw. Draw, highlight, blur and crop live on the screen during a demo or a lesson, not afterwards in an editor. Different job from annotating a saved capture.
  • Nothing leaves the Mac. No upload step, no account, no bucket to audit. For anyone capturing customer data or internal dashboards, that is a feature, not a limitation.
  • 50+ interface languages. If English is not your first language, this is not a small detail.
  • One menu bar icon, one updater, one licence key. The cost of ten small apps is not only the money.

What switching actually costs you

Be clear-eyed about it: moving from CleanShot X to Mac Kit means giving up scrolling capture, OCR and cloud links. There is no workaround in the app for the first two, and the third is deliberate — Mac Kit does not upload anything anywhere.

What you get back is $19 and thirteen tools. If you look at your menu bar right now and see a clipboard manager, a timer and a keep-awake app next to CleanShot, that maths works out. If CleanShot is the only utility up there and you use its advanced half, it does not.

Questions people ask

Is Mac Kit a CleanShot X clone?

No. Capture is one of fourteen tools, and it is deliberately the quick version: area, window, full screen, a light recording, a fast mark-up pass. If you want a capture app with a capture app’s depth, CleanShot X is that app.

Can I run both?

Yes, and some people should. Keep CleanShot X bound to your capture shortcut and let Mac Kit handle clipboard, timers, conversion and system controls. They do not fight over anything.

Does Mac Kit do scrolling capture or OCR yet?

No. Both are on the list of things we would like to build; neither ships today. We would rather you read that here than find out after paying.

Is $9.99 really one-time?

Yes. One payment, one licence key, one Mac, no renewal date. Every update in the 1.x line is included.

Try it before you decide

Mac Kit is a free 7-day trial of the whole app — no card, no account — then a one-time $9.99. If it does not replace CleanShot X for you, you have lost nothing but a download.
