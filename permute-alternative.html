Alternatives

A Permute alternative for the conversions you actually do weekly

Permute is the better media converter. If you transcode video with any seriousness, buy it. Mac Kit converts the boring everyday formats from the menu bar and spends the rest of its $9.99 on thirteen other tools.

The short answer

Buy Permute if video is the job: codecs, device presets, subtitles, quality settings you want control over. It is a specialist converter at $14.99 one-time and it is very good at being one.

Buy Mac Kit if your conversions are HEIC to JPG, a batch of PNGs down to one PDF, an audio file into something that will actually play, a PDF into Markdown — and you would rather not open a dedicated app to do it.

Both are one-time purchases. Neither rents you the software.

Where Permute is still the better buy

Permute has spent years on one problem and it is ahead of us on all of it:

  • Video. Formats, codecs, device presets, quality control. Mac Kit’s handling of video is basic by comparison and we are not going to dress that up.
  • Subtitles. Extract, embed, convert. Mac Kit does none of it.
  • Control. When you need to hit a specific bitrate or container, a dedicated converter is the right tool.
  • Drag-and-drop batching at scale. Built for queues of files, and it shows.
  • Intel Macs. Mac Kit is Apple Silicon only.

Where Mac Kit wins

Mac Kit’s converter is aimed at the conversion you do without thinking about it:

  • In the menu bar, not in an app. Convert and merge without breaking what you were doing.
  • PDF and Markdown, both directions. PDF to Markdown with the images pulled out, or Markdown to a polished PDF. Permute does not do this; for anyone writing documentation it is the single most useful conversion on the list.
  • Batch and merge for images, audio and documents. The everyday cases, done quickly.
  • Thirteen other tools. screenshots and recording, Screen Draw, colour picking, Mirror with studio light, file conversion, PDF↔Markdown, new file creation, Pomodoro, keep awake, Clean Mode, clipboard history, system monitor, Macros, and Quick Action.
  • Offline and account-free, in 50+ languages.

What switching actually costs you

Give up Permute and you give up real video capability. There is no clever framing that makes a menu bar panel equal to a dedicated transcoder, and if you convert video weekly you will feel it immediately.

If your last ten conversions were images, audio, PDFs and Markdown — which for most people they were — Mac Kit covers them from a shortcut and spends the remaining $5 difference on thirteen other tools.

Questions people ask

Which formats does Mac Kit convert?

Common image, audio and document formats, in batches, with merge for documents and images. Plus PDF to Markdown and Markdown to PDF.

Can Mac Kit convert video?

Only in a basic way. For anything where the codec, bitrate or container matters, use Permute.

Does it upload files to convert them?

No. Conversion runs on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded, and it works with no network at all.

Is $9.99 really one-time?

Yes. One payment, one licence key, one Mac, every 1.x update included.

Mac Kit is a free 7-day trial of the whole app — no card, no account — then a one-time $9.99. If it does not replace Permute for you, you have lost nothing but a download.
